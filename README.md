# Ayushi & Vishwas Suite

Create a premium, elegant, interactive Indian wedding invitation website for Ayushi & Vishwas, with the feeling of a luxury digital wedding invitation.

I have attached reference screenshots of an existing luxury digital wedding invitation. Use these only as inspiration for the design language, sophistication, typography, animations, interactive elements, card layouts and storytelling. Do NOT copy their exact layout, artwork, illustrations, text, branding or assets. Create an original design specifically for Ayushi & Vishwas.

Wedding Details

Bride: Ayushi
Groom: Vishwas
Location: Jamshedpur, Jharkhand, India
Venue: Hill View Resort, Jamshedpur

Wedding Schedule

Morning

24th — Sangeet & Engagement
Evening

25th — Wedding
Evening

The live countdown should count down to the Wedding on the 25th evening.

DESIGN DIRECTION

Think:

Luxury Indian wedding invitation × editorial fashion website × elegant digital stationery.

The website should feel like opening a beautifully designed physical wedding invitation, rather than visiting a generic wedding website.

Visual language:

 Warm ivory / cream background

 Deep burgundy / wine accents

 Dusty blush pink

 Subtle antique gold

 Delicate Indian floral/botanical motifs

 Elegant calligraphy/serif typography for major headings

 Sophisticated clean typography for body text

 Fine ornamental borders

 Lots of white space

 Soft paper/textural elements

 Premium editorial photography

Keep it romantic, sophisticated, timeless and slightly whimsical.

Avoid:

 Generic wedding templates

 Bright colours

 Cartoon-style graphics

 Excessive gold

 Overly ornate layouts

 Cheesy wedding clip-art

 Excessive animations

1. OPENING ENVELOPE

The website should initially show a beautiful closed wedding invitation envelope.

Minimal text:

AN INVITATION AWAITS

Ayushi & Vishwas

The envelope should have an elegant floral/Indian-inspired pattern and a subtle wax seal or monogram.

When the user clicks/taps it, the envelope should open with a beautiful, realistic animation, revealing the wedding invitation.

This should be the most memorable interaction on the website.

NAVIGATION

Keep navigation minimal and elegant:

HOME | SAVE THE DATE | OUR STORY | EVENTS | VENUE | TRAVEL

On mobile, use an elegant hamburger menu.

ANIMATION & INTERACTION

The website should feel alive but never gimmicky.

Use:

 Envelope opening animation

 Smooth scrolling

 Gentle scroll-reveal animations

 Subtle floating flowers/petals

 Elegant text transitions

 Image parallax

 Animated countdown

 Interactive event cards

 Interactive map

 Soft hover states

 Smooth section transitions

Animations should feel slow, luxurious and editorial.

MOST IMPORTANT

The attached screenshots are references for the quality and design sense, not templates.

Take inspiration from their:

Luxury + Indian wedding aesthetic + editorial typography + beautiful invitation cards + storytelling + interactive elements + subtle animation

But create a completely original visual identity for Ayushi & Vishwas.

The final experience should feel like:

“I just opened Ayushi & Vishwas's beautiful digital wedding invitation.”

and not:

“I opened a wedding website template.”

Make the website mobile-first, because guests will primarily open it through WhatsApp on their phones. Ensure it is equally beautiful on desktop and tablet.
